# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, in order. First-year students at BITS Pilani Goa deciding whether to walk into the induction interview; they arrive from Instagram on a phone, at night, curious and slightly intimidated. Second, recruiters, sponsors and alumni who land here from LinkedIn and need to believe this is a serious builder community in under ten seconds. Third, current members who come back for the inductees list, the projects log and the team page.

## Product Purpose

devsoc.club is the public face of the Developers' Society at BITS Pilani, K.K. Birla Goa Campus. It has to make a college club feel like a place with a night shift: six verticals (web, app, game, AI and ML, UI and UX, engine development), a flagship event (DevFest), fest websites and apps that the whole campus uses, and an induction that happens once a year. Success is a first-year who scrolls to the end and comes to the interview, and a sponsor who forwards the link.

## Brand Personality

Precise, unhurried, lit. Three physical-object words: a lit window in a dark building, a wall clock past midnight, a single work lamp. The site is one night on campus, dusk to dawn, and it speaks in short declarative sentences and never sells. The line it opens on: "The campus sleeps. Six rooms stay lit."

## Anti-references

The previous devsoc.club (teal SaaS template: icon cards, accordion FAQ, Lottie intro, light and dark toggle). Any AI-startup landing page: pill badges, eyebrows, three-column feature grids, purple gradients, glassmorphism, sparkles. Steampunk: copper-brown, Victorian ornament, goggles. Cyberpunk neon and glitch. Generic college-club sites with photo grids and "Join us" buttons.

## Design Principles

1. One dominant idea per viewport. Every screen has a single visual anchor and everything else supports it.
2. One object, not an icon. Each vertical is a single drawn object on a flat field with a name and a head, never a pictogram in a circle.
3. Motion is slow and explains. Every animation shows hierarchy, sequence or state, and only one thing moves on its own clock per viewport. Nothing bounces, nothing floats for decoration.
4. Real content only. Real projects, real names, real photos from the archive, real numbers set in mono. Nothing invented to fill a slot.
5. Works without the show. Every chapter is readable with JavaScript off, with reduced motion on, and on a phone in one hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum, AAA for body text on both grounds. Night is the default theme and a morning theme is one toggle away, kept across reloads. Full reduced-motion path: pins, scrubs and shader effects render as static compositions. Keyboard operable menu, theme toggle, flywheel index, shelf, inductee search, wall of windows and lightbox. Fine-pointer-only custom cursor, never on touch. Alt text written as captions, not labels.
