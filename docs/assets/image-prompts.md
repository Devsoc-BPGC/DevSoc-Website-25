# DevSoc 2026: Image Generation Prompts

## The idea in one paragraph

DevSoc is presented as an atelier of instruments. Goa was a port for five centuries; navigators came here with brass instruments. The society still ships. Every vertical is an instrument in the collection, photographed like a luxury watch campaign: brushed brass with verdigris patina, liquid chrome, obsidian glass, one soft teal key light in a black room, a faint warm amber rim light. No steampunk, no Victorian ornament, no copper-brown. Modern, minimal, expensive. The hero object is a hovering armillary sphere, the instrument that finds the way.

Generate at the largest size your tool allows. Target sizes below assume 1536x1024 landscape and 1024x1536 portrait, then upscale 2x. Save with the exact filenames listed. Drop files in the folders listed and the site picks them up.

## Consistency block

Paste this at the end of every prompt so all images look shot in one studio:

```text
Studio and grade: shot in a pitch-black studio with a matte obsidian floor that shows a faint soft reflection. One large soft key light from the upper left, coloured a muted patina teal (verdigris, like oxidised copper). A very faint warm amber rim light from the lower right kisses only the edges. Deep blacks, no visible light sources, no lens flare. Materials: brushed brass with subtle verdigris patina in the recesses, liquid mirror chrome, dark smoked glass. Camera: full-frame, 85mm lens, f/2.8, sharp focus on the subject, gentle falloff, fine 35mm film grain, slight halation on highlights. Colour palette limited to near-black, warm bone highlights, patina teal, brushed brass, one small amber accent. Photoreal render, hyperdetailed, luxury watch advertising quality. Absolutely no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no visible background objects, no rainbow gradients, no purple, no blue neon.
```
